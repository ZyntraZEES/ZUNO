Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Global Nova Technologies has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in ZUNO.

  • ZUNO is for players aged 13 and over.
  • Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors is forbidden by our Terms of Service.
  • Every player can report and block other players from inside the app (from a player's seat or profile). Party leaders can mute and remove players from their rooms.
  • We review reports, remove violating content, and permanently ban accounts involved in CSAE.
  • We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and cooperate with law enforcement.
