ZUNO by Global Nova Technologies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

ZUNO: Ultimate Card Battle ("ZUNO", "the app") is a card game made by Global Nova Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what the game needs to work: your account, your game progress, and what you choose to share with other players (your name, avatar, chat messages and voice). We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising trackers. You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Google sign-in: your Google account email address, display name and a unique account ID provided by Google.
  • Email sign-in: the email address you register with. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Guest play: an anonymous account ID with no personal details.

Profile and game data

  • Your username, player ID, chosen avatar, frame and card skin, level, wins, matches played, coins, gems, items you own, daily rewards and season progress.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, party (club) membership and role, and blocks.

Content you share

  • Text chat: messages you send in matches, party rooms, the global chat and private chats are stored so the other players can read them.
  • Voice chat: if you turn your microphone on in a party room or match, your voice is streamed live to the other players in that room. Voice is not recorded or stored by us. The microphone is used only after you allow it and turn it on yourself, and it switches off when the app goes to the background.
  • Gifts and reports: gifts you send, and reports you file about other players (including the reported content).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a confirmation of what you bought so we can deliver it; we never receive your card or payment details.

Technical information

Our service providers automatically process basic technical data needed to run the service, such as IP address, device type, operating system version and app version, for security and to keep online play working.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and save your progress across devices.
  • To run online matches, parties, friends, chat and voice chat.
  • To show leaderboards and public profiles (username, avatar, level and win record).
  • To deliver purchases and rewards.
  • To keep players safe: handle reports, mute or remove abusive users, and prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. What other players can see

Other players can see your username, player ID, avatar, frame, level, win record, online status, party and party role, and anything you post in chat or say on voice chat. Please do not share personal information such as your phone number, address or passwords in chat.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run ZUNO. They process data only on our behalf and under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): accounts, game data and chat. Firebase privacy
  • Agora: live voice chat. Agora privacy
  • Cloudflare: issues short-lived voice chat access tokens after checking you are signed in. Cloudflare privacy
  • Google Play: app delivery and in-app purchases. Google privacy

Data may be processed on servers outside your country. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and game data: while your account exists.
  • Party room and match chat: kept with the room and removed when the room is closed or cleaned up.
  • Reports: kept for up to 12 months to handle abuse and appeals.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account and profile data straight away. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. Messages you already sent to others in shared chats may remain visible to them, shown without your profile.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Microphone: you can deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time in your phone settings. The game works without it.
  • Block and report: you can block or report any player from their profile or seat.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing ahmiikhanx885@gmail.com.

7. Children

ZUNO is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Access to stored data is protected by Firebase security rules so that players can only read and change what they are allowed to. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
